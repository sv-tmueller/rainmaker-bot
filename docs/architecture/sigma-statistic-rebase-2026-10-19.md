# Appendix 2026-10-19: sigma-statistic re-basing measurement (#459, implements #435)

The #426 appendix accepted a statistic mismatch with the re-basing deferred to
#435: calibration cells are FITTED on the deterministic-archive sigma
(backfill.py's Previous Runs path supplies the inter-model std of scalar
deterministic forecasts; the archive has no ensemble members) while the live
path APPLIES the wider #426 extended-sigma mixture (all (source, model)
groups join the mixture, deterministic groups as singletons) into
apply_calibration's var_b term. This appendix measures that gap per
(station, variable, lead) and applies the pre-registered immateriality
threshold from #459.

## Method

Historical pools are reconstructed from the forecasts table (per-sample rows;
the #400/#417 method, precedent scripts/refit_v2_replay.py): one row per
(run, market, source, model) with the member values as a JSON list, so the
per-group composition the live fit_gaussian consumes is recovered exactly
(multi-value row = ensemble member set, single-value row = deterministic
model). Population: predictions with dist_params joined to settled outcomes,
collapsed to the latest run per (market, UTC day), temperature only,
Polymarket venue only (Kalshi stations are NCEI-blocked, a non-goal).

Each pool is reduced to two sigma statistics:

- fit arm (what the cells were fitted on): std across the deterministic
  models' values, i.e. the singleton groups' scalars. This mirrors the
  backfill archive statistic (one scalar per deterministic Open-Meteo
  model, no members).
- apply arm (what the live path feeds apply_calibration): the #426
  all-groups mixture variance, verbatim from fit_gaussian's ensemble arm,
  floored at MIN_SIGMA_F.

Both arms share the same mu (per-group-mean mean, identical in both
definitions) and the same settled actual, so the EMOS objective sees only
the sigma statistic differ. Each cell is then refit TWICE with the production
fitters (fit_calibration for TMAX, fit_student_t_free_df for TMIN,
CALIBRATION_FAMILY dispatch unchanged), and the fitted var_b compared:

- delta var_b = var_b(apply arm) - var_b(fit arm);
- predictive sigma evaluated at each arm's median ensemble_var;
- directional flip = var_b crosses zero, or the predictive-sigma ordering
  between the arms inverts anywhere over the observed ensemble_var range.

Tool: scripts/sigma_statistic_gap.py (kept beside refit_v2_replay.py;
read-only SELECTs, DSN via --dsn-file, never echoed). Results regenerate
with `uv run python scripts/sigma_statistic_gap.py`.

## Population

2971 (run, market) pools, 2026-07-18 through 2026-10-19 (the current regime's
history; the regime formally starts 2026-10-19 but the pools reconstruct the
whole window the cells' generation spans). 48 (station, variable, lead) cells
across 8 stations (ATL, BKF, DAL, HOU, LGA, ORD, SEA, SFO; the other cities
accumulated no settled pools in the window). 513 pools skipped for having
fewer than two deterministic groups (no spread to estimate on the fit arm),
4 for missing pool or lead. Cells with n >= 40: 30 (the threshold's scope;
lead 2 and lead 3 cells are mostly below it and exempt by pre-registration).

## Threshold application

Pre-registered rule (#459): immaterial iff abs(delta var_b) < 0.05 on every
cell with n >= 40 AND no directional flip in predictive sigma ordering.

Among the 30 eligible cells: 16 breach the abs(delta var_b) >= 0.05 bound
(max +0.517 at KDAL TMAX lead 0, max negative -0.443 at KSFO TMAX lead 1),
9 show a directional flip (including KDAL TMIN lead 0, where var_b moves from
the zero bound to 0.392, and KORD TMAX lead 0, 0.000 to 0.189). Union: 18 of
30 eligible cells breach. Median abs(delta var_b) among eligible cells is
0.067; the largest predictive-sigma move at the median pool is +0.939F
(KDAL TMIN lead 0), and the deltas are NOT one-signed (16 positive, 13
negative among eligible cells), so the mismatch is not a uniform
over-dispersion of live predictive sigma; it reshuffles var_a/var_b
allocations cell by cell, sometimes narrowing, sometimes widening.

Sensitivity arm: restricting the fit-arm det set to Open-Meteo models only
(excluding the NWS singleton; --fit-arm openmeteo) strengthens the result
rather than rescuing it: 21 of 30 eligible cells breach, median
abs(delta var_b) 0.121, max 0.610. The verdict holds under either fit-arm
boundary choice.

## Verdict: MATERIAL

The pre-registered threshold FAILS. Per the issue's decision rule this
package parks without expanding scope: no calibration behavior change ships
here, and the re-basing itself (rebuilding the fit path's sigma statistic on
live-shape pools, refitting all cells, bumping REGIME_SINCE, re-running
tail-check) is filed as a scoped follow-up issue (a full forecasts-table
historical-sigma reconstruction, size:L territory).

## Limitations

- The reconstruction population is settled-market pools, not the backfill's
  season-window pairs; both arms share it, so the deltas isolate the sigma
  statistic, but the absolute var_b values here are not the stored cells'
  values (the stored cells were fitted on the archive population).
- Only 8 stations accumulated settled pools since 2026-07-18; lead 2/3 cells
  sit mostly under the n >= 40 exemption, so the verdict rests on leads 0-1.
- TMIN history mixes the pre/post-#426 sampling eras; era effects cancel
  here because both arms consume identical mu and actuals.
- Lead is computed UTC-day-wise (run start date vs settlement date), the
  same convention as refit_v2_replay; late-evening runs read one lead higher
  than the live pipeline's local-calendar lead.
- var_a frequently compensates (it moves opposite var_b), which is why
  predictive sigma moves less than the var_b deltas suggest; the decision
  rule is on var_b as pre-registered, and the flips confirm the allocation
  between the two terms is statistic-dependent, not incidental.
- Proxy infidelity, disclosed for #462: the fit arm floors the
  reconstructed archive-style sigma at MIN_SIGMA_F (1.5F) before squaring,
  whereas the real fit path (backfill.py:249, consumed at :325) floors at
  1e-6. Tight-det pools therefore feed the EMOS objective a larger
  ensemble_var than the real fit ever saw, which can inflate delta var_b on
  low-spread cells. Breaches are dominated by wide-dispersion cells and the
  sensitivity arm strengthened the result, so the MATERIAL verdict stands,
  but #462 must not treat this proxy as exact.
